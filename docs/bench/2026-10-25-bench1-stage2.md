## Observations

- A2 (5845 MHz) sits exactly on Wi-Fi 169, its first choice; parking on 165
  means `esp_wifi_set_channel(169)` was refused under "US", as B7 parked on
  173 on 2026-10-24 suggested. A2 is therefore reached with a −20 MHz
  `phy_set_freq` pull, not on a public centre.
- No channel replied "tune failed": E8 (5945) held on 177 with the +60 MHz
  pull, D1 on 36 (UNII-1), L4 on 100 (DFS). Whether the synthesizer follows
  the pulls is not shown by a hold reply; that waits for a VTX (B1b).
- No held channel showed `"stuck":1` in the lines the user watched.
- Note added later the same day: the A2 pull is +20 MHz (5845 is above 165's
  5825), not −20 as written in the first observation above.
